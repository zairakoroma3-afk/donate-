Uplifting Hope.

Giving Hope. Building Futures. Transforming Communities.

Uplifting Hope Charity Organization supports orphaned, vulnerable, poor, and disadvantaged children and families, especially in Nise Community and surrounding communities in Nigeria.

Our work is focused on education, food and basic necessities, clothing and school supplies, healthcare support, mentorship and life-skills guidance, and support for vulnerable families and young mothers.

OUR FOUNDER

Chief Mrs. Josephine Nkiruka Okafor

Her desire to help others began at a young age after watching her mother share whatever little she had with people in need. That example of kindness stayed with her throughout her life.

Following her parents' memorial last year, she organized a Children's Day celebration for children in her community. Seeing their happiness led to a question that became the foundation of Uplifting Hope Charity Organization:

“If we can bring joy to children for one day, what can we accomplish if we support them throughout the year?”

OUR VISION

A Future Where Poverty Does Not Determine a Child's Future

We want every child to have the opportunity to stay in school, receive proper guidance, develop confidence, and grow into a responsible and productive adult.

A child supported today can become a leader tomorrow. READ OUR VISION →
MY DREAM

A Brighter Tomorrow for Every Child

  • No child leaves school because of poverty.
  • No child goes hungry.
  • Every orphan and vulnerable child knows that someone cares.
  • Families in need can find meaningful support.
  • Young people have positive opportunities and mentorship.
  • Every child believes that their future can be better than their present.
MY COMMITMENT

Turning Gratitude Into Service

Chief Mrs. Josephine Nkiruka Okafor sees this work as a way of showing gratitude to God Almighty for His faithfulness and for allowing her to become a blessing to others.

She invites individuals, families, community leaders, volunteers, organizations, businesses, and donors to join the mission.
