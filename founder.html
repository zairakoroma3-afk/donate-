OUR FOUNDER

Chief Mrs. Josephine Nkiruka Okafor

Founder & Visionary of Uplifting Hope Charity Organization

“My Mission. My Community. Our Children. Their Future.”

A Desire to Help That Began at a Young Age

My desire to help others began at a young age. I watched my mother share whatever little she had with people in need. She taught me that you do not have to be wealthy to make a difference. Her kindness became an example that has stayed with me throughout my life.

As I grew older, I continued helping people whenever I could, especially children and families facing difficult circumstances. After the passing of my parents, my desire to do more became even stronger.

The Children's Day Celebration That Inspired More

Following my parents' memorial last year, I organized a Children's Day celebration for children in my community. Seeing their smiles, excitement, and happiness touched my heart deeply. It made me realize how much a simple act of love and support can mean to a child.

“If we can bring joy to children for one day, what can we accomplish if we support them throughout the year?”

That question became the foundation of Uplifting Hope Charity Organization.

Why This Mission Matters

I have witnessed children struggle because their families could not afford school fees, food, clothing, books, and other basic needs. Some children leave school because of poverty, while others become vulnerable to gangs, crime, exploitation, substance abuse, and other harmful situations.

Often, these children are not choosing this life. They simply lack support, opportunity, guidance, and someone to believe in them.

I believe we must catch them young.

A Commitment Grounded in Gratitude

I am doing this not because I have everything, but because I am grateful to God Almighty for answering some of the secret prayers I have carried in my heart.

There were prayers I prayed quietly, dreams I shared only with God, and blessings I asked Him to give me so that I could one day be in a position to help others. Today, I see this opportunity as more than a personal achievement. It is a way of showing my gratitude to God for His faithfulness and for allowing me to become a blessing to others.

What God has placed in my hands, I want to use to touch lives.

Chief Mrs. Josephine Nkiruka Okafor

Founder & Visionary
